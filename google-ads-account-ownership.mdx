title: "Google Ads account ownership check"
description: "How to confirm that your business, not a supplier, owns your Google Ads account."
---

If a supplier set up your Google Ads account, it is worth checking who actually controls it. Losing access means losing years of conversion data and campaign history.

## Five checks

1. **Log in yourself.** Go to ads.google.com with a Google account your business controls. If you cannot see the account, you do not have access.
2. **Check your access level.** Under Admin, then Access and security, your own email should have Admin access, not Read only.
3. **Look at the billing.** The payments profile should be in your business name with your card or invoice details.
4. **Check the manager account link.** If an agency manages the account through its own manager account, that is normal. The problem is when the account was created inside the agency's account with no admin user from your side.
5. **Confirm conversion tracking ownership.** Your Google Tag Manager container and Google Analytics property should also list your business email as an administrator.

Google explains access levels in its help article on [managing access to your Google Ads account](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6372672).
