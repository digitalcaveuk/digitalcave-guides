A Google Business Profile is often the first thing a local customer sees, before your website. These are the parts worth your time, in order.

## 1. Primary category

Choose the most specific category that matches your main service. It has more effect on which searches you appear for than anything else you can edit. Add secondary categories only for services you genuinely offer.

## 2. Business name

Use your real trading name exactly as it appears on your signage and documents. Adding keywords to the name breaks Google's guidelines and can get a profile suspended.

## 3. Service area or address

If customers visit you, show the address. If you travel to customers, hide the address and set a service area instead.

## 4. Hours, phone and website

Keep these accurate, including holiday hours. Link the website field to the page that best matches the profile, usually the home page or a location page.

## 5. Reviews

Ask every happy customer, reply to every review, and never offer incentives for reviews.

## 6. Photos and posts

Real photos of your work and team help people choose. Posts are optional; a short monthly update is enough.

Google's help page on [how local results are ranked](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091) explains relevance, distance and prominence in plain terms.
