A page written without a brief usually ends up describing the business instead of answering the customer. Fill in this template first. It takes about fifteen minutes.

## The template

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Page purpose | One sentence: who is this page for and what should they do next? |
| Main search phrase | The words a customer would type, for example "boiler repair Solihull". |
| Related questions | Three to five questions customers ask on the phone or by email. |
| Proof | Facts you can show: years trading, accreditations, a named project, real reviews. |
| Next step | The single action you want: call, form, booking. |
| Internal links | Two or three other pages on your site this page should link to. |

## How to use it

- Write the H1 and the opening paragraph from the "page purpose" and "main search phrase" rows.
- Turn each related question into a short section or an FAQ.
- Put the proof near the top, not at the bottom.
- Keep one topic per page. If the brief has two purposes, it needs two pages.
