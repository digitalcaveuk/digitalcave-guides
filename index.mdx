[DigitalCave](https://digitalcave.uk) is an all-in-one web design, SEO and digital marketing agency based in the West Midlands, working with small businesses in Birmingham, Coventry and across the UK.

These guides are the checklists we use with clients, written so that an owner or office manager can work through them without a developer in the room. Each one takes ten to twenty minutes.

## Guides

- **Website launch checklist**: twelve checks to run before a new or rebuilt site goes live.
- **Google Business Profile basics**: the fields that matter for local search, and the ones you can ignore.
- **SEO page brief**: a one-page template for planning a service page before anyone writes copy.
- **Google Ads account ownership**: how to confirm that you, not a supplier, own your ad account and its history.

## Why we publish these

Most problems we see on small business websites are not technical mysteries. They are small, fixable gaps: a contact form that stopped sending, a page that nobody can find, an ad account the owner cannot log into. A short checklist catches most of them.

If you would rather hand the work over, our [web design services](https://digitalcave.uk/web-design) page explains how we build and look after sites.
