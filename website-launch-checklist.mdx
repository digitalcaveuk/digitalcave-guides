---
title: "Website launch checklist"
description: "Twelve checks to run before a small business website goes live."
---

Run these checks on the finished site before you point the domain at it, and again on the live address the day after launch.

## Content and contact

1. Every page says what the business does, and where, in the first two lines.
2. The contact form sends to an inbox someone reads. Submit a test and confirm it arrives.
3. Phone numbers and email addresses are clickable on a phone.
4. There is no placeholder text, no "Lorem ipsum" and no test pages left in the menu.

## Technical basics

5. Every page loads over HTTPS with no certificate warnings.
6. The site is not blocking search engines. Check that robots.txt does not say `Disallow: /` and that pages do not carry a `noindex` tag left over from the staging site.
7. Each page has its own title and meta description.
8. Images are compressed and have alt text that describes them.
9. Old URLs from the previous site redirect to their closest new page, so existing links keep working.

## Measurement

10. Analytics is installed once (not twice) and records a test visit.
11. Form submissions are tracked as conversions.
12. The site is submitted in Google Search Console with an XML sitemap.

Google's own [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) is the reference for what search engines expect.
